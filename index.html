AuraVoice — Evaluation & Judge Guide

🎯 What is AuraVoice?

AuraVoice is an accessible multimodal clinical agent designed for low-bandwidth and multilingual healthcare accessibility. It features zero-latency emergency triage (no false-suppress on negations), clinical prescription OCR, speech-to-text, and patient-to-doctor clinical handoff.

⚡ Recommended Judge Test Cases

1. Multilingual Emergency Triage: Try typing or speaking: "My chest has crushing pain radiating to my left arm" or "सीने में बहुत तेज़ दर्द हो रहा है".

2. Multimodal Prescription Check: Upload a medical prescription or medicine strip image under the Vision Analysis tab.

3. Voice Accessibility: Use the built-in microphone button to dictate symptoms in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or English.
